# Inviter Tracker Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 27 September 2026

Inviter Tracker ("the Bot") is a Telegram bot that helps groups and channels track invite activity, personal invite links, referrals, leaderboards, and welcome-message settings.

## Information the Bot processes

To provide these features, the Bot may process and store:

- Telegram numeric user IDs
- Telegram usernames and display names
- Telegram group, supergroup, and channel IDs
- Group/channel titles and chat type
- Personal invite links created by the Bot
- Referral attribution, including inviter ID, invitee ID, referral method, active/left status, and join count
- Per-chat welcome-message settings configured by administrators

## Information the Bot does not intentionally collect

The Bot does not intentionally collect or store:

- Telegram passwords
- Payment-card or banking details
- Private Telegram message history
- Contacts stored on your device
- Precise device location

Commands and button interactions may be processed temporarily so the Bot can respond, but ordinary message content is not stored as referral history.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate the Bot's features, including:

- attributing referrals and direct adds
- generating invite statistics
- displaying leaderboards
- preventing duplicate/rejoin referral abuse
- providing private dashboards
- managing group welcome messages

## Data sharing

The Bot does not sell personal data.

Data is processed through Telegram and the hosting/database services required to operate the Bot. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

## Data retention

Referral and chat records may be retained for as long as needed to provide invite history, anti-abuse protection, statistics, and leaderboard features.

A user or group administrator may request removal of applicable Bot data by contacting the Bot administrator through the Telegram community where the Bot is installed.

## Administrator responsibility

Group and channel administrators are responsible for informing their members that the Bot is being used for invite/referral tracking and for using the Bot in accordance with applicable laws and Telegram's rules.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored Bot data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact the administrator of the Telegram group or channel where Inviter Tracker is installed.
